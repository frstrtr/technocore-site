Our Solutions

Three technical verticals, one goal: reliable operational data.

DMCs run on fragmented supplier data — spreadsheets, emails and legacy systems. We turn that into structured, validated and automated workflows.

01

Data Integration & API Bridges

Synchronization of disconnected systems. Direct integration with industry platforms and reliable gateways for data exchange between DMCs and local suppliers.

  • SAMO & Technoheaven connectors
  • Supplier-to-DMC data gateways
  • Mapping, deduplication, sync monitoring
02

Workflow Automation & QC Logic

Replacing manual Excel processing with automated pipelines. Our proprietary Applouke Ingestion Engine classifies file patterns and enforces strict quality-control rules.

  • Price list & stop-sale ingestion
  • Pattern classification
  • Auto-Accept vs. Human-Review routing
03

Applied AI & RAG Architectures

Deployment of local large language models and Retrieval-Augmented Generation for intelligent search across a travel company's knowledge bases, itineraries and operating procedures.

  • Self-hosted LLM inference
  • Embeddings & vector search
  • Data stays within your infrastructure

About Us

From marketplace experiment to enterprise travel infrastructure.

Technocore Inc. (Delaware C-Corp) originated from testing dynamic marketplace solutions in the Mauritius travel sector. Recognizing a critical bottleneck in B2B data synchronization, we executed a strategic pivot.

Today, we focus entirely on resolving the technical debt of enterprise travel operators through custom automation.

Our long-term goal is a scalable, globally deployable IT infrastructure for the travel industry, and we intend to raise venture funding to accelerate it.
